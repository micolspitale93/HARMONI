Quickstart
================================

HARMONI 2.0 runs on ROS 2 (recommended for new projects, folder `ros2/`) and on ROS 1 Noetic (all the original packages, folder `ros1/`). Both are started with Docker through the `harmoni.sh` script; ROS 1 can also be installed natively on Ubuntu.
